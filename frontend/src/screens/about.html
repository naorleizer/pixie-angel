About Pixie

Pixie: My Financial Angel is an experimental assistant designed to help you master your money. By combining intelligent data analysis with intuitive conversational guidance, Pixie helps you uncover spending patterns and reach your financial goals.

Developed as an academic project at the Technion – Israel Institute of Technology, Pixie is a non-profit educational tool. Please note that the app is intended for personal exploration only; users are solely responsible for their financial decisions and any actions taken based on the app’s insights.
