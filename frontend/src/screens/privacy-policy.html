Privacy Policy

My Financial Angel (Pixie)

Last Updated: January 12, 2026

Welcome to Pixie. We believe that understanding your finances shouldn't come at the cost of your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

1. Our Role & Scope

This policy applies to the Pixie application.

The Project: Pixie is an experimental, non-profit educational tool, as part of our academic project at the Technion - Israel Institute of Technology.

Not Financial Advice: We are a support tool for budgeting and planning, not a licensed financial advisor or bank.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect what is necessary to help you manage your finances effectively.

A. Information You Provide

Account Data: Name/nickname, email.

Preferences: Your financial goals, risk comfort, and how you like Pixie to communicate with you (e.g., tone and "nudges").

B. Financial & Transaction Data

To provide insights, you may upload or import:

Transaction History: Merchant names, amounts, dates, and categories.

Budgeting Data: Your spending limits and savings targets.

Note: We do not require, and we recommend you do not provide, sensitive data such as full credit card numbers or bank account passwords.

IP Address: Used to determine a general location (e.g., City/Country) for currency or time zone settings.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

  • Analyze & Visualize: Categorize your spending and build your financial dashboards.
  • Personalize Coaching: Tailor Pixie’s advice and insights to your specific goals.
  • Improve the App: Fix technical issues and understand which features are most helpful for our research.

4. AI Processing & Models

Pixie uses Artificial Intelligence (LLMs) to provide conversational guidance.

Data Minimization: We aim to send only "summarized" data to AI providers (e.g., "Total spent on Food" rather than every single receipt) whenever possible.

No Training: We do not use your personal financial data to train third-party AI models.

Providers: We currently use Gemini. You can view their privacy terms https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms.

5. Sharing & Retention

No Sale of Data: We never sell your personal information.

Service Providers: We share data only with essential partners (e.g., cloud hosting or AI providers) who are contractually bound to keep it private.

Retention: We keep your data only as long as your account is active.

Transaction Data: Retained until you delete it or close your account.

6. Your Rights & Control

You have full control over your data and how Pixie interacts with you. Within the app, you can:

  • Choose Your Level of Personalization: You can use Pixie without sharing your personal interests, motivations, location, or communication style. While these details help Pixie provide a more tailored experience, they are completely optional.
  • Delete: Remove specific transaction data or permanently delete your entire account.
  • Correct: Update any inaccurate information in your profile.

7. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS) to protect your data during transit and store it in secure cloud environments. While we strive for maximum security, no system is 100% immune to risks.
